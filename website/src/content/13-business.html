Business calendars and holidays

These are two separate things. A business calendar covers weekends, working hours and half days. A holiday calendar is a set of specific dates. Compose them by passing the holidays into the business calendar’s options.

const holidays = createHolidayCalendar([
  { month: 1, day: 1, name: "New Year's Day" },
  { compute: (year) => ({ month: 5, day: lastMondayOf(year, 5) }), name: 'Memorial Day' },
]);

const cal = createBusinessCalendar({ /* weekend days, holidays, hours, half days */ });
addBusinessDays(cal, someDate, 5);

Business: isBusinessDay, addBusinessDays, subtractBusinessDays, differenceInBusinessDays, nextBusinessDay, previousBusinessDay. Holidays: isHoliday, holidaysBetween, nextHoliday, previousHoliday (capped at five years each way).

Country holiday datasets are out of scope. createHolidayCalendar is the abstraction, and filling it is your job.
