Serialization

Each format has a parse and a format function: ISO 8601 (parseISO), RFC 3339, RFC 2822, HTTP-date, and SQL DATETIME/TIMESTAMP. Epoch helpers cover seconds, milliseconds, microseconds and nanoseconds both ways (fromUnixSeconds, toUnixMilliseconds, …).

parseISO('2026-08-04T15:45:30Z');
fromUnixMilliseconds(1754321130000);
