Relative time

formatDistance(today, yesterday)                         // "yesterday"
formatDistance(today, yesterday, { numeric: 'always' })  // "1 day ago"
formatDistance(today, today.add({ days: 2 }), { locale: 'fr-FR' })  // "dans 2 jours"
formatDistanceToNow(threeHoursAgo)                       // "3 hours ago"

The direction is date1 - date2, so positive means “in X” and negative means “X ago”. Units switch at 60 seconds, 60 minutes, 24 hours, 30 days and 365 days. Override any of them with { cutoffs }. The months cutoff is in days because a month has no fixed length, and non-monotonic or non-positive values throw. It accepts PlainDate, PlainDateTime and ZonedDateTime, and throws on PlainTime.

formatRelative and formatRelativeToNow give calendar labels (“yesterday”, “last week”) instead of counts.
