Durations

formatDuration(duration, formatStr, options?) takes a Temporal.Duration or a plain field bag. Every unit has numeric, short and long forms, and the short and long ones are plural-aware.

UnitNumeric / short / long
yearsy yy yyy → 2 / 2yr / 2 years
monthso oo ooo
weeks, daysw ww www, d dd ddd
hours, minutes, secondsh hh hhh, m mm mmm, s ss sss
millisecondsS SS SSS
formatDuration({ hours: 2, minutes: 30 }, 'hhh mmm')                       // "2 hours 30 minutes"
formatDuration({ hours: 2, minutes: 30 }, 'hhh mmm', { locale: 'de-DE' })  // "2 Stunden 30 Minuten"

Zero-value units are omitted unless you pass { showZeroValues: true }, and cleaning up leftover separators is on you. An explicit locale: 'en-US' isn’t the same as omitting it, because Intl spaces things differently ("2 hr" vs "2h").

Also: formatDurationToParts, parseDuration, parseISODuration, formatISODuration, balanceDuration, totalDuration, compareDuration, addDuration, subtractDuration, roundDuration (months and years need a relativeTo).
